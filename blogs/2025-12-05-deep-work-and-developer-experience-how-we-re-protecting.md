In software engineering, “Deep Work” isn’t just a productivity buzzword — it’s a cornerstone of great Developer Experience (DX). When engineers have significant, uninterrupted time for focused work, they can solve complex problems more effectively, produce higher-quality solutions and find greater satisfaction in their day-to-day work. From our Developer Experience surveys, we’ve learned that the more engineers agree with the statement “I have significant time for deep and focused work during my work days” , the more productive and engaged our teams are.
