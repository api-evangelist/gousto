Introduction The Women of Silicon Roundabout conference is the UK’s largest Tech event for women. Gousto’s wonderful Chief of Staff, Louisa Golden suggested that a group of us attend the event together, using it as an opportunity to get to know our own Women in Tech community while learning about the industry and networking externally. About 30 Gousto employees signed up for WOSR 2023, from all areas of the business.
